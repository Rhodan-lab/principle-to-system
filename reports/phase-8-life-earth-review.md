## Review standard

Phase 8 applies the same coordinated review standard used for earlier phases. A module is promoted only when its `overview.md`, `technology.md`, and `explore.md` files pass together.

The review checks:

- scientific accuracy and appropriate uncertainty;
- mechanisms and causal chains;
- equations, variables, dimensions, signs, and validity conditions;
- model boundaries and scale transitions;
- source quality and exact source-to-ledger matching;
- safe, age-appropriate activities;
- metadata, prerequisites, connections, slugs, and status consistency;
- clear separation between observation, model, interpretation, and engineering claim.

Reviewed does not mean independently certified or release-ready.

## Module 13 — Cells and Bioenergetics

### Scientific corrections

- Reframed living cells as open, non-equilibrium systems that maintain organisation through matter and energy exchange while exporting entropy.
- Replaced the simplified ATP “energy currency” and “high-energy bond” framing with Gibbs-free-energy coupling and product-stability reasoning.
- Clarified that enzymes alter reaction pathways and kinetics but not equilibrium constants or overall reaction Gibbs free energy.
- Added catalytic-RNA scope instead of defining all enzymes as proteins.
- Distinguished direct metabolic-energy input from motion down an electrochemical-potential gradient.
- Corrected pyruvate oxidation versus citric-acid-cycle carbon accounting.
- Replaced “light-independent reactions” with a dependency-aware description of carbon fixation.
- Made Gibbs direction criteria conditional on stated thermodynamic constraints and explicitly separated direction from rate.
- Corrected the common claim that Michaelis constant is automatically an inverse affinity measure.
- Reframed cellular ATP yield as a model-dependent accounting result rather than one universal integer.
- Added transport direction, activity-ratio, membrane-potential sign, and ideal-solution limits to the electrochemical-potential equation.

### Technology and systems corrections

- Distinguished product separation from thermodynamic driving-force changes.
- Removed the claim that every overall bioprocess must simply be exergonic.
- Expanded design accounting to oxygen transfer, heat removal, redox balance, burden, growth, robustness, and genetic stability.
- Reframed biosafety around organism-, construct-, scale-, process-, and jurisdiction-specific risk assessment.

### Safety corrections

- Replaced hot-water handling with safely warmed water prepared by an adult or teacher.
- Replaced rigid or tightly sealed yeast systems with small, pressure-relieving observations.
- Removed the real-poison cascade question and replaced it with a non-operational electron-transport model.
- Added explicit instructions not to taste laboratory samples.

## Module 14 — DNA and Evolution

### Scientific corrections

- Replaced “exact duplication” with high-fidelity replication plus proofreading, repair, and residual error.
- Expanded gene expression to include functional non-coding RNA and multiple layers of regulation.
- Distinguished mutation as a source of new variants from recombination, segregation, gene flow, drift, and selection.
- Defined natural selection through heritable phenotypic differences and reproductive contribution rather than survival alone.
- Removed bacteria-specific DNA polymerase names from the universal replication account.
- Replaced fixed mutation and translation rates with context-dependent quantities and explicit measurement boundaries.
- Corrected allele-frequency notation so allele `A` is not automatically labelled dominant.
- Removed the assumption that relative fitness must inherently lie between zero and one.
- Reframed the central dogma to include non-coding RNA, reverse transcription, RNA genomes, and regulatory compatibility.
- Made PCR doubling an ideal efficiency limit rather than a guaranteed result.
- Replaced “silent mutation means no phenotype” with synonymous-substitution limits involving RNA structure, splicing, expression, and translation.

### Technology and systems corrections

- Added promoter, RNA-processing, codon-use, folding, modification, localisation, and toxicity constraints to cross-species expression.
- Replaced blanket antibiotic-marker language with regulated selectable-marker or chromosomal-integration choices.
- Clarified that PCR sensitivity and amplification depend on contamination, inhibition, primer design, efficiency, and stochastic sampling.

### Safety and privacy corrections

- Replaced real-family appearance analysis with anonymised or fictional pedigrees.
- Removed household rubbing-alcohol DNA extraction.
- Replaced the antibiotic-exposure prompt with a digital selection model.
- Directed genome exploration toward non-sensitive examples and evidence annotations.

## Module 15 — Ecosystems and Complex Systems

### Scientific corrections

- Removed the assumption that ecosystems generally self-organise into stable, enduring structures.
- Replaced the fixed “90% loss” and “10% rule” with variable transfer efficiencies and explicit accounting boundaries.
- Reframed carrying capacity as a changing model parameter rather than a fixed property.
- Qualified claims that modularity necessarily prevents cascading failure.
- Corrected the classical Lotka–Volterra model from a stable dynamic equilibrium to neutrally stable, structurally fragile closed orbits under ideal assumptions.
- Defined the logistic-map parameter as a dimensionless control parameter rather than a literal combined biological rate.
- Clarified that added ecological realism can stabilise, destabilise, damp, amplify, or qualitatively change dynamics.
- Replaced universal wetland-removal percentages with site-, design-, climate-, loading-, and monitoring-specific performance.
- Removed the assumption that ecological redundancy guarantees engineering reliability.

### Technology and systems corrections

- Replaced simple competitive-exclusion and carrying-capacity sizing with hydraulic, kinetic, stoichiometric, mass-transfer, community, and reliability constraints.
- Reframed closed ecological life support as regenerative material loops with leakage, accumulation, ageing, trace contaminants, and backup systems—not indefinite perfect closure.
- Corrected BOD as an operational characterisation of biodegradable organic loading rather than a class of molecules.

### Safety corrections

- Replaced direct contact with standing water and unknown organisms with non-contact observation or public data.
- Replaced the sealed jar containing living organisms with a diagrammatic material-and-energy accounting model.
- Replaced deterministic trophic-cascade storytelling with competing causal pathways and evidence requirements.

## Module 16 — Earth and Planetary Systems

### Scientific corrections

- Replaced mantle convection as the single primary driver with coupled slab buoyancy, ridge forces, boundary stresses, mantle flow, and lithospheric rheology.
- Reframed overturning circulation as a result of wind, tides, mixing, basin geometry, and buoyancy forcing rather than a standalone conveyor.
- Corrected radiative forcing to effective radiative forcing at the top of atmosphere after rapid adjustments and before surface-temperature response.
- Replaced “greenhouse gases are transparent to shortwave and trap heat” with wavelength-dependent absorption, emission, optical depth, and emission-level reasoning.
- Distinguished planetary energy imbalance from effective radiative forcing.
- Clarified that the zero-dimensional effective emission temperature is not a direct surface-temperature prediction or a complete no-greenhouse model.
- Limited the logarithmic carbon-dioxide forcing equation to an empirical approximation over stated atmospheric and concentration ranges.
- Distinguished initial-condition weather predictability from conditional long-term climate projections.
- Replaced fixed resolution-cost scaling and time-sensitive Argo fleet counts with architecture- and programme-dependent descriptions.

### Technology and systems corrections

- Separated weather forecast uncertainty, internal variability, model structure, parameterisation, forcing scenarios, and numerical choices.
- Reframed Argo as a changing international observing programme with core, Deep, and biogeochemical extensions.
- Replaced unstable supercomputer power claims with lifecycle boundaries involving electricity mix, cooling, utilisation, embodied materials, and replacement cycles.

### Safety corrections

- Replaced stove-based convection with browser simulation.
- Replaced permanent-marker globe activity with digital tools or removable tape.
- Replaced terraforming instructions with non-operational radiative-transfer comparison.
