# Product Alpha UI/UX Research Note

## Decision

Redesign the learner experience as a guided learning cockpit while preserving the five-step route, local-only learner state, exact-revision evidence boundary, and zero repository mutation.

## Research translated into design

- Keep complex learning learner-paced and segmented into explicit steps.
- Make the learner's current location and progress continuously visible.
- Keep one dominant reasoning task visible beside the current explanation.
- Provide large, separated targets and high-contrast focus indicators.
- Keep focused headings and controls clear of sticky navigation.
- Preserve reduced-motion and forced-colors behavior.

## Implemented experience

- Compact orientation hero with a clear start action and experience boundaries.
- Sticky semantic progress indicator and five-step journey navigation.
- Visited and active step states.
- Previous and next step controls generated from the canonical route order.
- Stronger separation between reading, active reasoning, and private reflection.
- Mobile horizontal journey navigation and single-column task flow.
- No external fonts, assets, analytics, accounts, persistence, or uploads.

## Validation boundary

This change improves interface structure and usability. It does not claim measured learning effectiveness or product-market fit. Those claims require separate observation and evaluation.
