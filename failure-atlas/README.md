# Failure Atlas

The Failure Atlas studies recurring failure patterns rather than collecting dramatic accidents. Its purpose is to show how ordinary mechanisms, delayed signals, hidden couplings, and reasonable local decisions can combine into system-level failure.

## Failure story structure

1. Normal operation
2. Disturbance or change
3. Hidden condition
4. Amplifying mechanism
5. Detection delay
6. Threshold crossing
7. Failure propagation
8. Protective barriers
9. Why the barriers failed
10. Redesign options
11. New risks introduced by the redesign
12. Transfer to other domains

## Pattern families

- positive feedback and runaway behaviour;
- resonance and repeated loading;
- thermal runaway;
- cascading network failure;
- brittle optimisation and lost redundancy;
- sensor drift and false confidence;
- common-mode failure;
- queue growth and congestion collapse;
- corrosion, fatigue, and accumulated damage;
- human-machine interface mismatch.
