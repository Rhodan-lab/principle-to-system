# Design Challenges

Design challenges ask learners to make and defend engineering choices under measurable constraints. They are not craft instructions and do not assume one perfect solution.

## Required structure

1. Need and context
2. Stakeholders
3. Requirements and success measures
4. Hard safety constraints
5. Assumptions
6. System boundary
7. At least three concept alternatives
8. Minimum quantitative model
9. Trade-off matrix
10. Failure modes and safeguards
11. Safe test plan
12. Selected concept and rationale
13. Evidence that could change the decision
14. Sources and module links

Use [`templates/design-challenge.md`](../templates/design-challenge.md).
