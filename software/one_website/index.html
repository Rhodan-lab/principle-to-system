02 / A METHOD, NOT A COURSE

Understanding is an investigation.

Principia guides you from observation to redesign. Atlas provides a separate, exact-revision window into research evidence. Models are useful when you know what they leave out.

Try the five-step investigation ↗
  1. 01
    Observe

    Begin with a real surprise, not a formula.

  2. 02
    Map

    Locate boundaries, stores, and flows.

  3. 03
    Model

    Predict first. Experiment with a simulation.

  4. 04
    Diagnose

    Find failure patterns and the assumptions that matter.

  5. 05
    Redesign

    Make a better decision under constraints.

03 / THE EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

One website.
Two distinct authorities.

Principia owns learning, simulation, pathways, and its own publication status. Atlas owns pinned knowledge identity, provenance, and review metadata. The website connects the experiences without pretending that one automatically validates the other.
